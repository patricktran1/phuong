## Google: not independently retrieved

Actual Google review text and a current Google aggregate rating were unavailable in this session. Do not describe the website as already displaying verified Google reviews. No fictional Google review content or rating is bundled into the client.

The optional adapter uses Google's official API, not scraping:

- Place Details (New): https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-details
- Places attribution and review policies: https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies
- Google Maps terms: https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/
- Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The adapter requests business identity and review fields, validates the identity, and retains author/source information. Review excerpts are limited to 24 words, with an ellipsis for truncated text. They are selected by Google's relevance ordering, not sorted to suppress low ratings. The availability UI is hidden until the owner's server settings exist. Synthetic unit-test fixtures are not customer content and are never served by the application.
