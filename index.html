Privacy & terms.

This website provides general information and an email-draft tool. Viewing a piece or preparing an inquiry does not create an order, reservation, or quote. Confirm availability, specifications, prices, and service arrangements directly with the shop.

This website does not store inquiry form entries or process payments. The email builder creates a draft on your device. Sending it uses your email application and the receiving email service.

Archive photography is served by this website. Fonts and optional Google photographs and profile images are requested from third-party hosts. Those hosts receive normal connection information, such as your IP address. Links to Yelp and Google take you to those services, where their privacy policies apply.

When available, choosing “Show Google reviews & photos” requests reviews and up to two photos from Google through the website’s server. No inquiry form information is included in that request.

Google Maps content is subject to Google’s Terms and Privacy Policy. For questions about this shop, call (510) 835-8288.
